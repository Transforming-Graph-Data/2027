Nelly Barret is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at INSA Lyon and the LIRIS lab, Lyon, France, since 2025. She defended her PhD in 2024 at Inria Saclay, France, on integrating, summarizing and exploring heterogeneous datasets represented as a graph for efficiency and generality. She is currently the co-supervisor of 2 PhD students (I-DEMO EXPERTMEDIA 2026 and ESKER CIFRE) and the main suepervisor of several students on neurosymbolic AI with a strong emphasis on the symbolic part. Her research interests span heterogeneous data management, data integration, graph data, and data exploration, possibly for more transparent, trustworthy and frugal decision making. Nelly is the co-organizer of the 2027 edition of the CORIA-TALN conference and has been the co-organizer of the CMLS@ER workshop. She has PC and review responsabilities in major database conferences and journals, including VLDB Proceedings, PLoS One, WWW, and CIKM.
